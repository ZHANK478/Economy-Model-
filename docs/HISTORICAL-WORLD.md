# Historical external conditions for the UK scenario

The historical calendar changes the external economy independently of UK policy and the game's seed. GDP for foreign markets is rebuilt from their existing population and productivity model with date-based impulses. A separate set of impulses affects external orders, trade costs, supplies, world prices, interest and the pool of international capital. UK GDP, prices, employment, bank losses and policy remain outcomes of the existing simulation. Tourism and travel are approximated using the service-export sector. The numerical impulses and their recovery paths are model assumptions, informed by the direction, geography and relative severity of the historical episodes. They are not historical estimates of a counterfactual or a month-by-month reproduction of observed data.

The institutional event in Paris appears as news only. The Iraq War and allied foreign decisions are external events. Brexit, UK pandemic restrictions, joining the euro and British military participation are left to the UK player. Legacy political saves switch on the next turn, retain their past journal and only receive news about events starting after the migration. Existing external conditions still in effect on the migration date apply from then on.

## British responses

When the UK has a government, its player can choose how to respond to the Iraq request, the Syrian refugee crisis, the Huawei restrictions and the Hong Kong security law. Military participation in the Iraq coalition is modelled as a Commons motion. A player-led cabinet controls the governing parties on that motion. Under a rival cabinet, the player controls only their own party, and the ordinary roll-call system computes other votes with a motion-specific preference. The other three UK responses are executive decisions.

The dates refer to the contemporary diplomatic request or policy window. The rates for external commitments, resettlement and equipment replacement are scenario estimates. External spending settles through the existing treasury and foreign accounts. Resettlement feeds into population and migration, and replacement raises the temporary price of imported equipment. Voter reactions depend on the issue, voter preferences and recorded party positions. The programme amounts are modelling choices, and the simulation covers commitments at the national level rather than named deployments, individual visa applications or individual operators.

## European Union accounts

The Brussels dossier aggregates the member economies. UK production and population enter the dossier while the UK is a member. The foreign trade partner always contains only external members. From revision 28, the historical enlargements transfer each entrant from the residual market to the EU market, including its estimated share of British trade. Market growth compares a common country perimeter around those transfers.

Opening GDP comes from the [World Bank GDP series](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CD). The accession calendar follows the [Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/how-enlargement-works/timeline-accession-eu-member-states/). The 13 additional countries use rounded population anchors and simulated real GDP paths. The French, German and Italian dossiers show separate 2000 fiscal benchmarks from [IMF Country Report 01/203, Table II.3](https://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/scr/2001/cr01203.pdf). The [European flag](https://european-union.europa.eu/principles-countries-history/symbols/european-flag_en) is drawn as a local SVG.

## British foreign programmes

The British scenario can branch into euro and Schengen integration or an overseas trade programme inspired by the UK's [Global Britain review](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/the-integrated-review-2021). The calendar for proposed British decisions is counterfactual. Commons votes use the ordinary roll call. Negotiation durations and reductions in bilateral transaction costs are scenario assumptions, while Japan, Australia and Canada are based on actual UK agreements.

The integration route follows the [Commission's convergence criteria](https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/euro/enlargement-euro-area/convergence-criteria-joining_en) and the [Amsterdam Treaty protocols](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/treaty/ams/sign/eng). Its Commission dialogue represents negotiations requiring the Council, ECB or participating governments as appropriate. The game uses annual CPI, a fiscal deficit and debt screen, a borrowing-cost proxy, and 24 months inside a 15% band. Schengen is represented by a travel and services cost channel. Euro adoption moves monetary policy to selected [ECB refinancing rates](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/key_ecb_interest_rates/html/index.en.html) and anchors the domestic settlement unit to the [euro's dollar rate](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/AEXUSEU). Domestic ledgers retain their internal unit to preserve stocks and contracts. Published data are coarsened to the model's monthly resolution.
